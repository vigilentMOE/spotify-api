Why this is a worksheet and not a script. The Spotify Web API has no endpoint for playlist folders — it cannot read, create, or move them — so the moves have to be made by hand. It also cannot see your personalised Mix and Radio playlists at all, so their genres were derived from the playlist name and, for Radio playlists, from the seed track's artists.

Folders

Percentages show how much of a playlist's genre signal falls in the assigned family — 100% means every genre found pointed the same way. Bins holding fewer than three playlists are pooled into Misc; playlists whose genres split evenly land in Mixed, and ones with no genre signal at all in Unclassified.
